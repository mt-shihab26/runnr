# Assets

Candidate 3D asset packs for runnr from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets). All Kenney assets are [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), so they can be used in the game without credit.

Browse the full [3D category](https://kenney.nl/assets/category:3D) rather than the keyword search, which doesn't filter by keyword.

## Prototyping

| Pack                                                    | Used for                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Prototype Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/prototype-kit) | Blocks, ramps and markers to lay out the track before picking a theme |

## Plan

1. Get running, jumping and the obstacle pool working with the Prototype Kit.
2. Switch to the city street theme with Animated Characters Protagonists. The city packs share one style, give many obstacle types (cars, barriers, trains) and their road pieces work as repeating track segments.

## Downloading

On a pack's page, click **Download** (the price is optional, 0 works). Use the files in the `GLB format` folder, raylib loads them directly with `LoadModel` and `LoadModelAnimations`.
